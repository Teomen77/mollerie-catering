# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui, motion. Deploy target named in the brief: GitHub repository `mollerie-catering` and Vercel production.

## Users

Inferred: people and hosts in Köln who are planning a gathering and need a buffet or catering. They are addressed with formal Sie. The brief does not name a narrower audience.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site for mollerie so a visitor can understand the offer in Köln-Ehrenfeld and ask for a quote by phone or email. Success is a call or a written enquiry, not an online order.

## Positioning

mollerie cooks buffets and catering in Köln-Ehrenfeld. Published stance, from mollerie.de: food should give pleasure even in a plain form; high-quality basic products, preferably from the nearby region; no convenience food, ready-made fonds, or flavour enhancers; fusion only in measure.

## Operating Context

The kitchen is at Liebigstraße 145, 50823 Köln. Enquiries go by phone or email. The old site describes an unverbindliches Gespräch about the occasion and wishes, then an individual offer. No online shop, no published opening hours.

## Brand Commitments

- one-prompt mode; direction assigned by concept-seed, not picked by the user.
- Brief-pinned look: Framer-level editorial, warm food photography, appetizing and clean. Subtle motion. The hero is visible at once. No “Willkommen auf unserer Homepage” tone.
- The wordmark stays lowercase: mollerie.
- Voice is formal Sie, direct, and specific. No welcome-page filler.

## Product Principles

- Only published facts and the brief. If a number, dish, or hour is missing, leave it out.
- The enquiry is the product: phone and email stay one step away.
- Appetite comes from photography and plain sentences, not from a fake menu.
- Legal pages stay short and name what is still unpublished.
