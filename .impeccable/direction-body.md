## Direction contract

THESIS: This surface is one invitation to ask for catering, not a homepage. It refuses a welcome line, a published menu, and a row of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Uncoated paper is the ground of the whole page. A warm food photograph is the front of the card. A paper band overlaps that photograph. Ink is warm near-black; beet ink is only the enquiry action. Petrona sets the invitation line, Figtree the facts. Corners are sharp. Depth is overlap.

STORY: The visitor sees food, understands buffets and catering in Ehrenfeld, and calls or asks for an offer. Prices and dishes stay off the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed buffet photograph under a paper letterhead. A paper band overlaps the lower edge. The heading is “Buffets und Catering in Ehrenfeld.”, then one sentence, then “Angebot anfragen” and the landline. All of it is visible on load. The photograph drifts slowly from a slight scale and is never hidden. No eyebrow.
